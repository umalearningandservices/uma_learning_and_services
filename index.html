Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer both online and offline classes? ▾
Yes. We provide a complete hybrid learning system with both online and offline training options. Offline sessions are conducted in Jaipur with hands-on guidance, while online classes are structured with live interaction, recorded support, and doubt-solving sessions. Students can choose flexible batches based on their availability.
Is job assistance guaranteed after a course? ▾
We provide strong 100% job assistance support which includes professional resume building, interview preparation, mock interviews, and direct referral opportunities. However, final placement depends on the candidate’s performance, skill level, and market requirements. Our focus is to make every learner industry-ready.
What programming languages and technologies do you teach? ▾
We offer industry-focused training in Python, JavaScript, PHP, HTML, CSS, SQL, and modern frameworks depending on the course path. Our curriculum is designed based on real project requirements, ensuring students learn practical skills used in companies and startups.
Do you provide live project training? ▾
Yes. Every student works on live industry-level projects such as ERP modules, CRM dashboards, automation tools, and business systems. This ensures practical exposure and helps build a strong portfolio for job placement or freelancing.
Do you provide certification after course completion? ▾
Yes. After successful completion of training and assessments, we provide industry-recognized completion certificates. These certificates validate your skills and help strengthen your profile for job applications and freelancing opportunities.
Can beginners join your courses? ▾
Absolutely. Our courses are designed for beginners as well as advanced learners. We start from fundamentals and gradually move to real-world project development, ensuring every student can learn effectively regardless of prior experience.
Do you support freelancing and business setup guidance for students? ▾
Yes. We guide students in freelancing, client acquisition, project handling, and even starting their own IT or automation-based business. Our mentorship helps learners become independent professionals.
Do you offer weekend or flexible batches? ▾
Yes. We provide flexible batch timings including weekend, evening, and customized schedules to suit working professionals, students, and business owners.

Why Small Businesses Should Automate Their Workflows Early

How custom automation — from attendance to CRM — saves hours every week and reduces costly manual errors.

22 Jan 2026 •
Read More →

Most small businesses put off automation until they feel "big enough" to need it. In practice, the earlier you automate the repetitive parts of your operations, the more time and money you save while the business is still small enough for those savings to matter most.

The usual starting points are the tasks nobody enjoys anyway: attendance and leave tracking, reminder systems for renewals and compliance dates, basic CRM follow-ups, and internal help-desk style ticketing so requests don't get lost in someone's inbox.

None of this requires an expensive enterprise system. A well-built, custom-fit automation layer — connected to WhatsApp, email, or your existing tools — usually pays for itself within the first few months through hours saved and errors avoided.

We build these systems end-to-end, with no advance payment and lifetime support, so you can see the impact before committing further. Reach out and we'll map out where automation would help your business first.

Learning Tips

Online vs Offline Classes: Which Should You Choose?

Both formats work — here's how to decide based on your schedule, learning style, and career goals.

4 Feb 2026 •
Read More →

There isn't a universally "better" option between online and offline classes — the right choice depends on how you learn best and what your week actually looks like.

Offline classes suit people who want hands-on guidance in the room, immediate doubt-solving, and the structure of a fixed schedule. It's often the better fit for hardware-heavy or highly practical tracks, and for learners who focus better away from home.

Online classes suit people juggling a job, college, or family responsibilities, since you can attend live sessions and fall back on recordings if you miss one. It also works well if you're comfortable being self-directed between sessions.

Many of our students actually mix both — offline for the sessions that benefit from in-person practice, online for everything else. Our team can recommend a format once we understand your schedule and the course you're aiming for.

How to Prepare for Interviews After a Certification Course

Practical tips on building your portfolio, resume, and interview confidence after finishing your training.

18 Feb 2026 •
Read More →

Finishing a certification course is only half the job-search equation — how you present what you've learned is what actually gets you shortlisted and hired.

Start with a small portfolio: even two or three real project examples (a spreadsheet automation, a sample website, a design mock-up) demonstrate your skills far better than the certificate alone.

Keep your resume specific — list the tools and tasks you can actually do, not just the course title. Interviewers respond much better to "built an automated attendance tracker using Google Sheets and Apps Script" than to "completed automation course."

Finally, practice explaining your projects out loud before the interview — confidence in describing your own work matters as much as the work itself. Our placement support includes resume review, mock interviews, and referrals, so you're not doing this alone.
